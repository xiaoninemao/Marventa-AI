# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Marventa AI community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Focus criticism on ideas and implementations, accept responsibility for mistakes, and respect differing viewpoints. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
